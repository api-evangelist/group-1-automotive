## Browse Inventory (HTML - Best for Web Browsing LLMs)
Server-rendered HTML pages with vehicle data. No JavaScript required.

Browse All Vehicles: https://www.group1auto.com/llm/inventory/
Browse SUVs: https://www.group1auto.com/llm/inventory/?bodytype=SUVs
Browse Trucks: https://www.group1auto.com/llm/inventory/?bodytype=Trucks
Browse Vans: https://www.group1auto.com/llm/inventory/?bodytype=Vans
Browse Sedans: https://www.group1auto.com/llm/inventory/?bodytype=Sedans
Browse New: https://www.group1auto.com/llm/inventory/?type=new
Browse Used: https://www.group1auto.com/llm/inventory/?type=used
Browse Under $30k: https://www.group1auto.com/llm/inventory/?price_max=30000
Browse 2022+: https://www.group1auto.com/llm/inventory/?year_min=2022

Parameters: type, make, model, year_min, year_max, price_min, price_max, bodytype, keyword, limit, page
